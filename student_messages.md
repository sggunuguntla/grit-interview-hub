# Student-facing messages

These are the three primary messages shown in the student journey.

## 1. Eligibility unlocked
“Congratulations! You've completed all the required skills for this Track Badge. You're now eligible to book your live interview.”

## 2. Booking confirmation
“Your interview has been successfully scheduled. Please join using the interview link at least 5 minutes before your scheduled time.”

## 3. Interview passed
“Congratulations! You've successfully cleared your Track Badge interview. Your badge is now ready to be awarded.”

## Supporting product copy used in the prototype
- Retake: “Your interviewer has recommended a retake. Review the feedback and book another available slot when you're ready.”
- Failed: “Your interview result is marked as not passed. Please review the feedback and contact your programme coordinator if you have questions.”
- Malpractice review: “A review has been opened for your badge. Your interview result remains unchanged while the case is under review. We'll update your dashboard when a decision is recorded.”
